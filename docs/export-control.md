The **infernal-law** project is classified as **EAR99** under the U.S. Export
Administration Regulations (EAR).

EAR99 items generally do not require an export license for most destinations
and end users. Restrictions can still apply based on the destination, end
user, end use, sanctions, embargoes, or other applicable law. Anyone exporting,
re-exporting, or transferring the project is responsible for checking the
rules that apply to the specific transaction.

This classification covers the project as reviewed on the date above. Reassess
it when functionality, cryptography, dependencies, intended use, or applicable
law materially changes. Third-party components may have separate licensing and
export-control obligations.

This notice records the project's classification and is not legal advice.
